## Croissant

Define a small-batch dough, separate dough and lamination butter, and include egg wash.

Название: Croissant → Croissant. Порции: 6-8 servings → 6. Время: 365 → 363 мин (оценка).

| Ингредиент | Количество | Уточнение |
|---|---:|---|
| bread flour | 200 g |  |
| butter | 100 g | 20 g for the dough; 80 g for lamination |
| instant yeast | 4 g |  |
| whole milk | 120 ml |  |
| sugar | 20 g |  |
| salt | 4 g |  |
| egg wash | 10 g | beaten whole egg; amount used for brushing |

Предлагаемые шаги:

1. Mix the flour, yeast, milk, sugar, salt and 20 g softened butter into a smooth dough. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour.
2. Flatten the remaining 80 g cold butter between sheets of baking paper into a small square. Chill until firm but pliable.
3. Roll the dough around the butter to enclose it. Roll into a rectangle and fold in thirds. Chill for 30 minutes. Repeat the rolling, folding and chilling 3 more times, keeping the butter cold.
4. Roll the dough about 4 mm thick and cut into 6 long triangles. Roll each triangle from its base to its tip and place on a lined baking tray.
5. Leave to proof for about 2 hours at room temperature, until visibly puffy. The butter should not melt during proofing.
6. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Brush with 10 g beaten egg and bake for 15–18 minutes, until deeply golden. Makes 6 small croissants.
